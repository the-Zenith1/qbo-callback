ML Maintenance QuickBooks integration

A private connection between this company's own systems and its QuickBooks Online company file. It is not offered to the public and there is no sign-up.

Access is granted through Intuit's consent screen to the administrator of the company file. To disconnect, open QuickBooks Online, go to Settings, then Apps, and remove the app. That revokes the tokens immediately.
