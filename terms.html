Terms of use and privacy notice

Applies to the private QuickBooks Online integration operated by ML Maintenance. Last updated 22 November 2026.

What this integration is

A private, unlisted integration that connects to a single QuickBooks Online company file belonging to the operator. It is not offered to the public, has no customer sign-up, and is used only for the operator's own accounting administration.

Terms of use

  • The integration is for the operator's internal use only. No third party is granted access.
  • Access is authorised through Intuit's OAuth consent screen and can be revoked at any time from QuickBooks Online under Settings, Apps.
  • The integration is provided as is, without warranty. The operator is responsible for reviewing any record it creates or changes in QuickBooks Online.
  • These terms may change. The current version is always this page.

Data the integration accesses

  • Accounting records in the authorised company file: customers, suppliers, invoices, bills, payments, items, accounts and reports.
  • No bank credentials, no card numbers, no payroll data, and nothing from any other QuickBooks company.

How the data is used and stored

  • Used only to read records and, where instructed, create or update them in the same company file.
  • Held on infrastructure controlled by the operator, access limited to the operator.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest on that infrastructure and are replaced automatically as they rotate.
  • Data is not sold, shared, published, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • No third-party tracking or cookies on this site.

Retention and deletion

Cached records are kept only while needed for the tasks that requested them. Revoking the app in QuickBooks Online stops all further access, and stored tokens can be deleted on request.
